Rangka OACP

Turns a corruption risk assessment into a complete Organisational Anti-Corruption Plan project paper in Word.

A Malay-language tool that guides officers step by step through the AD01, CRA01 to CRA05, PT01 and OACP framework forms. It tracks how complete each form is, saves every plan privately to the user's account, and generates the project paper as a Word document in the browser. A draft can be downloaded at any time; the final version unlocks once every form is complete.
